Privacy

Last updated October 2026

This page explains what happens to the information you send through the Book and Hire Will forms on captureheaven.com.

What I collect

Only what you type into a form: your name, email, position, ministry or church name, website, the service you need, and any dates, location, or details you add.

How I use it

I use it only to read your request and write back to you. I don’t sell it, rent it, or add you to a mailing list.

Who else handles it

  • Form submissions are delivered to my inbox through a form service (Formspree). If the form opens your own email app instead, your message is sent from your email account.
  • The site is hosted by Vercel, and its fonts load from Google Fonts. These services may record basic technical details, like your IP address, as part of running their service.

Cookies

This site doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

To ask what I have from you, or to have it deleted, email wkittle@captureheaven.com.
